Our Apps

Card Grading AI

Automatic grading for collectible cards using AI, with visual analysis of corners, surface, and centering. Get a grade estimate and a clear report on the main factors affecting card value in seconds.

  • Advanced visual analysis of corners, surface, and centering
  • PSA/BGS-style grade estimate
  • Detailed report with strengths and areas for improvement

Book Writing AI

AI writing assistant that supports the full creative process—from defining the concept and building the outline to writing chapters, stylistic revision, and narrative consistency checks.

  • Outline and chapter generation
  • Narrative consistency checks
  • Stylistic text revision
  • Export to multiple formats

Card Centering Pro

Precise centering measurement with a guided overlay and percentages on all four sides. An essential tool to quickly assess card alignment before grading.

  • Millimeter overlay for accurate measurement
  • Left/right and top/bottom percentages
  • Screenshot saving for comparison and archive
